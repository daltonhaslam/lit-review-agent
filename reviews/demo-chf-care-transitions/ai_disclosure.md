# AI-Assisted Review Disclosure (Demo)

**Review type:** systematic
**Completed:** July 2026

> **This is a demo review**, illustrating the `ai_disclosure.md` artifact that `/lit-review`
> writes automatically at the end of every completed review. Wording below is representative,
> not a verbatim excerpt from a real review's disclosure.

This review was produced using the `lit-review-agent` Claude Code skill. Abstract screening,
full-text eligibility assessment, data extraction, quality appraisal, citation-chasing candidate
screening, gap analysis, and evidence synthesis were AI-assisted (performed by Claude inside the
Claude Code conversation). Database retrieval, deduplication, PDF download, and evidence-level
classification were fully automated (rule-based or API-driven; no LLM involved).

A human reviewer approved the review at each of its checkpoints: PICO formulation, protocol lock,
post-retrieval review, library retrieval, final included-set approval, and the iteration decision,
plus a final review of the executive summary and output package.

**Limitations:** screening and extraction decisions were made by a single AI reviewer per pass, not
independently adjudicated by a second human reviewer; full-text assessment for articles without an
available PDF relied on abstract-level text only. As with any AI-assisted output, findings,
citations, and extracted data should be independently verified before use in any clinical,
academic, or administrative decision.
